- Now, **My favourite project this month:** [anshulbadhani/kiki](https://github.com/anshulbadhani/kiki)
    - Kiki is basically a chotu sa ball which lives on your desktop. 
    - You can ask it questions, annoy it and her mood depends on how we treat her.
    - Suppose we are bullying her, she will get angry and give dry replies, get sarcastic or maybe even insult. But she's still helpful. So, you better be nice to her.
    - Kiki uses State Machines under the hood to simulate its complex behaviours
    - and the best part? You can add custom python scripts called `skills` to kiki's home folder and kiki will pick those up in the real time. No need to recompile and it uses approximate search for blazingly fast results. So, you can have an arsenal of skills for kiki and she will find the best one in no time.
